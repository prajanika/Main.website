About Prajanika

Prajanika is a science communication and public health awareness initiative dedicated to improving understanding of women's health across critical stages of life. Through evidence-based infographics and educational resources, the project seeks to bridge the gap between scientific knowledge and community awareness by making complex health information accessible, engaging, and easy to understand.

Women experience unique physical, emotional, and social transitions throughout their lives. Yet many important health concerns remain poorly understood, under-discussed, or surrounded by stigma. Adolescence, motherhood, and menopause are periods marked by significant biological and psychological changes, often accompanied by questions, challenges, and misinformation.

Adolescent Health

Addressing puberty, menstrual health, nutrition, and body image.

Postpartum Mental Health

Awareness on maternal mental health & postpartum depression.

Menopause & Aging

Exploring hormonal transitions and healthy aging strategies.
