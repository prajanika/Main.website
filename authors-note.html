Authors’ Note

Health, dignity, and well-being are fundamental rights that should be accessible to everyone. Yet, many issues affecting women across different stages of life continue to remain surrounded by silence, misunderstanding, stigma, and inadequate access to reliable information.

Through Prajanika, we seek to contribute to a more informed, empathetic, and supportive society by presenting scientifically accurate, easy-to-understand, and culturally sensitive infographics on some of the most important health challenges faced by adolescent girls, mothers during the postpartum period, and women undergoing menopause.

Key Focus Areas

Adolescence: Empowering adolescents with knowledge to promote confidence and healthier futures.

Postpartum Period: Raising awareness about postpartum depression to encourage empathy and early care access.

Menopause: Normalizing discussions around menopause and empowering women through this transition.

"Together, let us continue building a future where knowledge promotes health, where empathy dismantles stigma, and where every woman has the opportunity to thrive with dignity and confidence."

— Team Prajanika
